# Day 25 — AI Industry Article Reference

## Article / Report

**Title:** 2026 AI Index Report

**Publisher:** Stanford Institute for Human-Centered Artificial Intelligence (Stanford HAI)

**Year:** 2026

**Source:** Stanford AI Index

---

## 1. Why This Source Was Selected

The Stanford AI Index is a reliable research source that studies the development, adoption, economic impact, technical progress, and future direction of Artificial Intelligence.

The 2026 report provides current information about AI adoption, AI agents, AI skills, investment, productivity, and the changing AI job market.

---

# 2. Technology Discussed

One important technology discussed in the report is **AI Agents / Agentic AI**.

AI agents are systems that can go beyond simply answering questions. They can perform multi-step tasks, use tools, interact with software, and coordinate different actions to achieve a goal.

The report shows that AI agents improved significantly on computer-use benchmarks during 2025, although they still fail a meaningful portion of structured tasks.

The report also highlights the growing importance of agentic AI and orchestration skills in AI job postings.

---

# 3. Business Impact

AI is becoming increasingly important in business.

According to the 2026 AI Index, organizational AI adoption reached **88% in 2025**, while generative AI was being used in at least one business function by around 70% of organizations.

AI is being used to improve:

- Customer support
- Software development
- Marketing
- Business operations
- Research and analysis
- Automation

The report also identifies measurable productivity improvements in several structured business tasks.

However, organizations still need to consider reliability, cost, security, responsible AI, and workforce changes when deploying AI systems.

---

# 4. Impact on Developers

The growth of AI is changing the skills expected from developers.

Developers increasingly need to understand not only traditional machine learning but also how to build and operate AI-powered systems.

Important skills include:

### AI Skills

- Machine Learning
- Deep Learning
- Generative AI
- Large Language Models
- Prompt Engineering
- RAG
- AI Agents
- Tool Calling

### AI Engineering Skills

- LangChain
- LangGraph
- API Integration
- Model Orchestration
- Evaluation
- Testing
- Logging
- Monitoring

### Software Engineering Skills

- Python
- Git/GitHub
- FastAPI
- Docker
- Cloud Deployment
- Databases
- Security

The report indicates that demand is shifting toward skills required to coordinate and operationalize AI systems rather than only general familiarity with chatbots.
