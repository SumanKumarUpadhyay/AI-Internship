# Prompt Comparison

## Task

Generate a professional internship email.

---

## Prompt 1

Write an internship email.

### Output

Very short email with minimal details.

---

## Prompt 2

Write a professional internship email for an AI/ML role including my skills.

### Output

Email became more relevant and professional.

---

## Prompt 3

Write a formal internship email for an AI/ML Engineer position. Mention my Python, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Flask, FastAPI, and project experience. Keep the email under 150 words.

### Output

Generated a complete, structured, and role-specific email.

---

## Observation

Each prompt became more specific, resulting in clearer and higher-quality outputs.
